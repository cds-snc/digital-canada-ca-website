<p>Today, I am&nbsp;very pleased&nbsp;to share the&nbsp;<a href="https://digital.canada.ca/reports/annual-report-2025.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>2025–2026</strong>&nbsp;<strong>CDS Annual Report, Strengthening End-to-End Digital Service Delivery</strong></a>, and&nbsp;the <a href="https://digital.canada.ca/reports/tactical-plan-2026.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener"><strong>2026–2027</strong>&nbsp;<strong>Tactical Plan, Powering Service Delivery at Scale</strong></a><strong>.</strong>&nbsp;</p>



<p>Together, these documents reflect the progress we have made over the past year and the priorities that will guide us as we enter the final year of the&nbsp;<a href="https://digital.canada.ca/reports/strategy-2024.pdf" data-type="link" data-id="https://www.bing.com/ck/a?!&amp;&amp;p=d80f5dbdce662e12df0c62ce08137b08dc8e734d4f18e5d969fcc76d7bdabd8cJmltdHM9MTc4NjQ5MjgwMA&amp;ptn=3&amp;ver=2&amp;hsh=4&amp;fclid=3125d6b2-2c3e-6dc5-066e-c1012df46c57&amp;psq=cds+next+strategic+vision&amp;u=a1aHR0cHM6Ly9kaWdpdGFsLmNhbmFkYS5jYS9yZXBvcnRzL3N0cmF0ZWd5LTIwMjQucGRm" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">CDS Strategic Vision 2024–2027</a>.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<p>As we look ahead to CDS’s 10th anniversary, we remain focused on what matters most; delivering better outcomes for the people who use Government of Canada (GC) services. Across government, our Platform products <a href="https://articles.alpha.canada.ca/forms-formulaires/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">GC Forms</a>, <a href="https://notification.canada.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">GC Notify</a>, and <a href="https://design-system.alpha.canada.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">GC Design System</a>, developed as digital public goods that are reusable, interoperable, and designed to scale, continue to be adopted at a rapid pace.<br><br>We launched <a href="https://login.canada.ca/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">CanadaLogin</a> and began the transition to replace the dozens of login systems currently used across government. Meanwhile, our on-demand digital expertise services are supporting multiple departmental service delivery initiatives. Together, this work enables departments to deliver secure, reliable, and user-centered digital services more effectively.  </p>



<p>CDS has also been instrumental in strengthening&nbsp;<a href="https://digital.canada.ca/2025/06/23/digital-public-infrastructure-dpi--powering-the-gcs-digital-future/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Canada’s Digital Public Infrastructure</a>&nbsp;(DPI), the shared systems that enable trusted, seamless digital services across governments and beyond.&nbsp;</p>



<p>We advanced Canada’s Digital Public Infrastructure on two fronts: by leading cross-sectoral roundtables in collaboration with the&nbsp;<a href="https://dgc-cgn.org/about/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Digital Governance Council</a>&nbsp;that included other governments, the private sector, academia and civil society from across Canada, and by&nbsp;launching GC Issue and Verify with our first partners in the federal family, a new platform that enables the secure issuance and verification of digital credentials that has vast potential for use cases of both other governments and sectors across the country.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<p>This progress&nbsp;demonstrates&nbsp;the dedication of our teams, the strength of our partnerships, and our commitment to improve end-to-end digital service delivery across the GC.&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<p>As we complete the final year of our&nbsp;CDSNext&nbsp;Strategic Vision, we will continue to turn our vision into action, strengthening digital services, supporting our partners, and building more connected, seamless, and responsive government delivery for the public.</p>
